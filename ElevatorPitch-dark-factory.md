Dark Factory: Lights Out, Nobody Watching
=========================================

## Abstract

Part 3 of the Dark Factory series. We saw factory blog posts appear in the
beginning of 2026 and the big question was "But how???". Turns out that once you have done
parts 1 & 2, the factory is suddenly within reach.  
Expect a slowdown first: you'll have to build the factory, the dashboards, and
add more and more autonomous agents that all take over some part of what the
human in the loop used to do. Keep watching and tweaking until you feel confident,
then turn off the lights.


## Target Audience

Developers already running 4-6 coding agents in parallel who are wondering what comes after
the merge queue.

## Key Takeaways

- Roles, not prompts: architects, implementers, a lander and an observer, each with one job
- The human talks only with the architects, all other agents work autonomously
- The architects turn all work into a task graph (beads), ready for the pipeline
- Anchor the agents to a reference application: a live codebase as the source of truth
- Going dark is an ongoing effort: stuck detection, cost per bead, sandboxing, rollback, dashboards, ...

## Session Format

20 minutes
